# Medrael AI

Medrael AI is an open-source AI visibility and website intelligence platform.

Analyze a website and understand how prepared it is for AI-powered search, including crawler accessibility, structured data, content quality, brand visibility, trust signals, and content freshness.

## What it checks

- AI crawler accessibility
- Structured data
- Content readability
- Brand footprint
- Trust signals
- Content freshness
- AI/LLM visibility

The platform generates a 0–100 score, an overall grade, and a prioritized action plan.

## Why Medrael AI?

Search is changing.

People increasingly discover businesses through AI-powered systems such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI features.

Medrael AI helps businesses understand how visible and understandable their websites are to these systems.
